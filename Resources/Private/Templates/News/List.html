conn_base override of georgringer/news-twb5's Templates/News/List.html: adds a "Template Layout" switch (settings.templateLayout, options defined in Configuration/Sets/News/page.tsconfig via tx_news.templateLayouts) so editors can pick between the original list-group rendering (1 = Liste) and a Bootstrap card grid with 2, 3 or 4 cards per row (values 2/3/4 = Karten), matching the site's other card-style content elements. The numeric value doubles as the row-cols-lg-{n} class, so no separate "columns" setting/field is needed.
